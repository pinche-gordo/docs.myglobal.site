MYGLOBAL.site connects public profiles you can discover and share. You can view a profile without creating an account. Some loyalty or commerce features depend on each business’s program, configuration, and eligibility requirements.

## Choose how you want to get started

### I want to learn about a business or professional

[Explore public profiles on MYGLOBAL.site](https://www.myglobal.site/buscar). Open a profile to learn what it offers and how to contact it.

### I want to explore products or services

[Explore Agora](https://agora.myglobal.site/explore), the marketplace for products, services, memberships, reservations, and experiences from independent businesses. If an action requires consumer account access, use the **Sign in** option shown in Agora. See the [Agora guide for consumers and members](/en/agora/).

### I want to join as a member

- **You already have an account:** [Sign in to MYGLOBAL.site](https://www.myglobal.site/signin) and follow the options shown to open your member panel.
- **You are starting onboarding:** [Create your profile](https://www.myglobal.site/signup). Anyone can start with a free membership. Member onboarding requires choosing a sponsor: this relationship affects collaboration settings, and your sponsor receives a commission if you upgrade to Pro. Choose the person who invited you or someone you expect to work with; don’t select a random profile. Then generate the temporary link and continue from it on this or another device.

**Commission note:** MYGLOBAL.site is not a multilevel marketing program. If you upgrade to Pro, the commission goes to your direct sponsor only. There are no commissions through additional levels or for collaboration groups; consultants do not earn commission simply for supporting several groups.

Consumer access in Agora and member onboarding in MYGLOBAL.site are separate flows. If you only want to view public profiles, you can start without registering.
