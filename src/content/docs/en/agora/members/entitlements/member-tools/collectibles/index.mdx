## An opportunity for creators

The idea is to develop a digital collectibles marketplace, similar to an NFT marketplace, where artists and businesses can offer digital copies or collections of their work. For example:

- a photographer could offer digital copies or collections of their images;
- a musician could offer digital editions of their work;
- a business could pair a physical product with a digital piece or collectible.

The direction includes direct payments between buyer and creator (P2P), with x402 integration. The platform does not charge a marketplace commission on these sales. The design also aims to let each owner keep digital assets in their own wallet.

## Current status

This experience is still in development. The marketplace concept and payment model do not mean that creating, listing, buying, transferring, or delivering collectibles through Agora is available today. Do not use this page as instructions for minting or selling an asset.

If you are interested in this possibility, tell us in the chat on this page.
