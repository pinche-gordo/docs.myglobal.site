Digital collectibles are assets associated with participant wallets. They are not Agora catalog offer types or merchant modules. Availability and actions depend on the asset and enabled feature.

See the [digital collectibles guide](collectibles/). For Check-in and other Rewards tools, visit [Rewards for members](/en/members/rewards/).
