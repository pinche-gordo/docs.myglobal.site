## What it is

Real Estate is a separate Agora merchant module. It is not a Catalog product type. When the module is enabled for a business, authorized staff can open its Real Estate workspace from Agora administration.

## What you can manage

The current workspace brings together tools for:

- **Properties:** create and maintain property records.
- **Media:** organize images and other files used by the business.
- **Listings:** prepare and manage real estate offers linked to properties.
- **Agency memberships:** review agency relationships available in the workspace.
- **Leads:** record and follow up with interested contacts.
- **Viewings:** coordinate property viewings and configure availability.
- **Clients:** maintain client records and link them with leads.

Available data and actions depend on the business configuration and permissions.

## Open the workspace

1. Sign in and open the business administration in Agora.
2. Select **Open Real Estate** in the V2.5 administration.
3. Use the Real Estate workspace menu to choose a section.

If the option is missing or access sends you back to administration, ask the business administrator to confirm that Real Estate is enabled.

## New workspace in development

A newer Real Estate workspace is under development and review. For now, this guide describes the workspace opened through **Open Real Estate**. The newer workspace has not replaced that entry point.
