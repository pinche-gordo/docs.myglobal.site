Modules enable separate business workspaces. They are not catalog offer types.

- [**Booking**](/en/agora/members/entitlements/modules/booking/): a separate booking module, distinct from native MYGLOBAL.site appointments.
- [**Real estate**](/en/agora/members/entitlements/modules/real-estate/): a separate module for real-estate operations and listings.

Availability depends on enabled capabilities and business configuration. Detailed guides will be published after each workflow is verified.
