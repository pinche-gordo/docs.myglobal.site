Catalog offer types are separate from merchant modules. Availability and limits depend on each business's configuration and enabled merchant capabilities.

- [**Menu**](/en/agora/members/entitlements/catalog/menu/): food and drink offers.
- [**Products**](/en/agora/members/entitlements/catalog/products/): physical, digital, or externally fulfilled products.
- [**Services**](/en/agora/members/entitlements/catalog/services/): service offers and deliverables. Booking is a separate module.
- [**Subscriptions**](/en/agora/members/entitlements/catalog/subscriptions/): recurring offers; catalog definitions do not mean enrollment, billing, or renewal is available.
- [**Access passes**](/en/agora/members/entitlements/catalog/access-passes/): time-based, limited-use, or conditional access; defining a pass does not guarantee customer issuance or redemption.

Step-by-step guides will be added as each workflow is verified.
