Members can use the Agora tools enabled for their business and access. MYGLOBAL.site membership and Agora merchant capabilities are separate: having Pro does not mean every Agora feature is enabled.


For the available business capabilities, see [member capabilities and tools](/en/agora/members/entitlements/).
