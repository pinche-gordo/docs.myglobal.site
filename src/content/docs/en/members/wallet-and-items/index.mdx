The **Wallet & items** workspace brings together wallet linking, digital-asset views, and tools to manage or create tokenized items when available to your account.

## Topics in this area

- **Overview:** wallet status and links to Web3 tools.
- **Wallets:** linking and viewing supported balances.
- **Digital items:** view and manage profile assets.
- **Create assets:** minting tools and linking assets to items where applicable.
- **Tokenization plans:** options for organizing asset-related plans.

Wallet and blockchain actions may require a compatible wallet, a specific network, and explicit confirmation. Check each workflow’s requirements before signing or submitting a transaction. Digital items may appear alongside [Rewards](/en/members/rewards/), while each area keeps its own functions.
