**Insights** groups metrics and activity that help you understand how people use your profile and what is happening across your network.

## Views

- **Overview:** key indicators and performance context.
- **Traffic:** available visit and source data for MYGLOBAL.site and Agora.
- **Network:** recommendations and referral activity available to your account.
- **Activity:** recommendations and other recent records.

Data depends on its source, the selected period, and each integration’s availability. A missing metric does not necessarily mean there was no activity.
