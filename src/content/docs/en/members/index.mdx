This section explains how to use your MYGLOBAL.site member account tools. **Home** helps you review items that need attention and open frequent actions. The **Member Panel** organizes settings and tools into workspaces.

## Explore the panel

- [**Member home**](/en/members/dashboard/): pending items, frequent shortcuts, collaborators, and an activity summary.
- [**Profile**](/en/members/profile/): identity, public description, contact, presence, media, and privacy.
- [**Connected**](/en/members/connected/): setup and status for linked services.
- [**Operations**](/en/members/operations/): appointments, collaborators, workflows, cases, and infrastructure.
- [**Insights**](/en/members/insights/): traffic, network, and activity.
- [**Wallet & items**](/en/members/wallet-and-items/): wallet, digital assets, and related tools.
- [**Rewards**](/en/members/rewards/): benefits and campaigns available to eligible members.

Tool availability can depend on your role, plan, profile, and connected services. Check the guide for each workspace before changing settings that affect your profile or integrations.

For merchant tools in Agora, see the [Agora member guide](/en/agora/members/).
