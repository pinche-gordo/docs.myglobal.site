**Operations** brings together the work you do with your services and collaborators. Account and connection setup lives in [Connected](/en/members/connected/); Home provides shortcuts to frequent tasks.

## Operations areas

### Appointments

Review requests, the calendar, and availability for MYGLOBAL.site appointments. These appointments are separate from Agora bookings.

### Team

Manage linked collaborator profiles or start the flow to create and link a new profile. Review what the flow will do before confirming creation.

### Workflows and cases

Review work routing and notification setup, and manage support cases when the tool is enabled.

### Infrastructure

Manage installations or nodes connected to your account, where applicable.

Controls may require linked services and specific permissions. Each workflow guide will explain its requirements and the changes it makes.
