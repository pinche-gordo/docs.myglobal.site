Member home is a quick view of your account. It brings together useful information to help you decide what to handle and open your tools; it does not replace detailed profile settings or the Member Panel workspaces.

## What you may find

- **Pending items:** requests and notices that need attention when the relevant source is available.
- **Key metrics:** summary data such as profile views and activity.
- **Collaborators:** links to connected profiles and their management tools.
- **Tools:** shortcuts to services enabled for your account.
- **Profile actions:** share or open your public profile.
- **Rewards and Web3 Hub:** summaries and related entry points; each tool keeps its own workspace and requirements.

## If information is missing

Some cards depend on connected services or data that is still loading. If a summary is unavailable, open the related workspace to check its connection or setup status.
