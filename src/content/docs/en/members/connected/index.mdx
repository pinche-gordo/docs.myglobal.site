**Connected** brings together options for linking services, reviewing the identity they use, and checking whether a connection is ready. Setting up a connection is different from using the operational tools that depend on it.

## Connected areas

### Communication

Setup and status for communication services such as Zulip, Mastodon, and Telegram. Daily shortcuts and operational actions may live in Home or Operations.

### Commerce

Agora linking, SSO readiness, available permissions, and links to tools managed by Agora.

### Payments

Stripe Connect status and linking options for collaborators. Some resale or membership capabilities are still in development; check what is available before offering them.

### Work management

Vikunja connection setup and status. Project assignment, routes, and notifications are covered in Operations.

Available services and actions depend on your permissions, account status, and each provider’s configuration.
