Rewards is a MYGLOBAL.site member entitlement available to Pro members and higher. It operates independently of Agora: members can use Rewards without having an Agora merchant catalog.

## Check-in

[Check-in](/en/members/rewards/check-in/) is part of Rewards. Use it to manage campaigns and display their check-in QR codes from the MYGLOBAL.site panel.
