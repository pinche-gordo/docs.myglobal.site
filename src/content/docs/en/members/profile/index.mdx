The **Profile** workspace configures your account information and how your public profile appears. Review changes before saving: some settings affect what other people see on your profile.

## Profile areas

### Basics

Manage your **handle**, display name, branding, and profile description. The handle is part of your public address; the display name is the name shown on your profile.

### Trust and media

Review verification tools and manage media available to your profile. Verification details are private and should not be shared in screenshots.

### Contact

Configure contact links, appointment availability, share links, and your vCard.

### Presence

Manage social links, locations, and specialties that describe where you are and what you do.

### Privacy

Review options for submitting and tracking requests related to your personal data.

Detailed guides for each area will be added as we review the current forms and their effects. Visible options can vary with your profile state and permissions.
